GyosJS Pipes

Transform values with built-in pipes

Currency Pipe

Price (USD): {price | currency('USD')}
Price (VND): {price | currency('VND', 'vi-VN')}
Price (EUR): {price | currency('EUR', 'de-DE')}

Date Pipe

Today (MM/DD/YYYY): {today | date('MM/DD/YYYY')}
Today (DD/MM/YYYY): {today | date('DD/MM/YYYY')}
With time: {today | date('DD/MM/YYYY HH:mm:ss')}

String Pipes

Original: {text}
Uppercase: {text | uppercase}
Lowercase: {text | lowercase}
Capitalize: {text | capitalize}
Slug: {text | slug}
Truncate (20): {text | truncate(20)}

Chained Pipes

Slug + Uppercase: {text | slug | uppercase}
Truncate + Uppercase: {text | truncate(15) | uppercase}

Number Pipe

PI (0 decimals): {pi | number(0)}
PI (2 decimals): {pi | number(2)}
PI (5 decimals): {pi | number(5)}
